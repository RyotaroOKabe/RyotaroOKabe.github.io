<a href='https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03795'>Paper</a> /

An LLM agent inverse-designs ion-implantation recipes for spatially programmed materials by using a physics-based ion–matter simulator as a tool: given a target depth profile, it selects ion species, energies, angles, and target layers, runs and interprets simulations, and iterates until the profile matches, with minimal human input.
