Fast-ion implantation of Nb-doped Cd3As2 thin films enhances the negative longitudinal magnetoresistance signature of the chiral anomaly by more than 100% relative to pristine films while maintaining crystallinity, establishing high-energy implantation as a route to chiralitronic properties.
