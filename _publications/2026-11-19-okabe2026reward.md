Disagreement between predefined reward scores and expert judgment, studied for electronic flat bands, is turned into a discovery signal: expert preferences learned from pairwise comparisons guide a crystal diffusion model toward compounds such as KCo2As2 and CoGa that a numerical bandwidth score would discard.
